**Figure 5 | Frozen internal states contain information predictive of human gloss judgments beyond physical and color cues.** **a,** Internal-state extraction and supervised readout. The multimodal model weights remain frozen. Selected visual blocks are spatially averaged, bridge visual tokens are averaged, and language blocks contribute the final prompt-token state before answer generation. V6–V24 and L9–L36 denote the sampled visual and language blocks; B denotes the vision–language bridge. Pale backgrounds distinguish the three stages. Each representation undergoes a fixed, label-free Gaussian projection to 128 dimensions, training-set standardization and ridge regression. Human responses are supervised targets for the readout only, not for multimodal-model training or feature extraction. All 3,888 stimuli are evaluated using the existing 12 shape-group or illumination-group folds. Ridge regularization is selected from {1, 10, 100} on a fixed group-disjoint inner validation subset; test-group responses are excluded from all fitting. The stage diagram summarizes the principal path; Qwen3's additional DeepStack branches are retained separately in f.

**b,c,** Human-judgment readout performance across all sampled common stages under shape holdout (b) and illumination holdout (c). Lines connect discrete sampled representations and do not imply a continuous processing trajectory. Shaded bands show pointwise 95% crossed shape–illumination bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples of fixed out-of-fold predictions. Vision and language stages use different pooling rules. Late language states provide higher readout performance than the sampled early-language state in both models, but the profiles are not monotonically increasing through all stages. **d,e,** Human prediction combining the physical/color baseline with an internal-state residual readout under the same shape and illumination splits. Within each training split, a nuisance model uses categorical physical gloss c and chroma, plus sine/cosine hue terms at the first and second harmonics. Ridge regression predicts its human-response residual, and the held-out nuisance prediction is added back. Gold dashed lines show nuisance-only baseline R² (0.261 for shape holdout; 0.272 for illumination holdout). The displayed curves and intervals evaluate the combined prediction against the original human responses; they are not residual-only R² values. Nuisance fitting is repeated within the inner and outer training splits.

**f,** Full readout atlas for human matched gloss, physical gloss c and the same model's behavioral reference-matching value. Each cell reports held-out R²; S and I denote shape and illumination holdout, respectively. All nine common sampled states are shown for both models, along with Qwen3's three additional DeepStack merger outputs (D0–D2, zero-based hook identifiers). The three targets have separate supervised ridge fits. A common color scale supports visual comparison; differences between target rows are descriptive and are not presented as a formal selectivity test. **g,** Complete image-level human readout predictions from the final sampled language block (L36) under shape holdout, selected by architectural position rather than best test performance. Each plot contains 3,888 held-out predictions. Hexagon color shows image count on a common logarithmic scale; dashed lines denote exact prediction. Human readout R² is 0.589 for InternVL and 0.668 for Qwen3. Predictions are shown on their fitted scale, including values outside the reference range; no clipping or post-hoc rescaling is applied.

**h,** Added predictive information from the final language state over the physical/color baseline. The statistic is ΔR² = R²(combined nuisance + residual readout) − R²(nuisance only), using paired predictions for the same held-out images. Gains are 0.325 (95% interval 0.245–0.407) and 0.378 (0.301–0.457) under shape holdout, and 0.332 (0.253–0.414) and 0.370 (0.294–0.451) under illumination holdout, for InternVL and Qwen3 respectively. These intervals were calculated from 2,000 paired crossed-condition bootstrap samples (seed 20261004), preserving the same shape and illumination multiplicities for baseline and combined predictions. Both predictions remain fixed during resampling; the variance denominator is recomputed in each sample. Baseline predictions were reconstructed from the archived stimulus metadata and original training-only regression and verified against the archived baseline R² values. Intervals quantify uncertainty over the sampled stimulus conditions conditional on the fixed outputs and analysis, not variation across human participants or model training runs. Together, the figure establishes decodable information relevant to human gloss judgments; causal use of a particular internal direction requires separate intervention evidence.
